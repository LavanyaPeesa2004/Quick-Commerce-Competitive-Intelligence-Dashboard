# Quick-Commerce-Competitive-Intelligence-Dashboard
Interactive Power BI dashboard benchmarking Blinkit, Zepto and Swiggy Instamart across revenue, orders, delivery efficiency, customer satisfaction, discounts and city-level demand.


## 🎥 Interactive Dashboard Demo

The dashboard supports dynamic filtering across platform,
city, product category, payment method and discount status.

[Watch the interactive dashboard demo](https://lnkd.in/p/d4R8sTwi)


## 📊 Power BI Portfolio Project

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to benchmark
Blinkit, Zepto and Swiggy Instamart across key business,
operational and customer experience metrics.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to compare the competitive
performance of three major quick-commerce platforms and
identify areas of strength and potential improvement.

The analysis focuses on:

- Revenue
- Orders
- Average Order Value
- Delivery efficiency
- Customer satisfaction
- Discount effectiveness
- City-level performance
- Product category demand
- Customer age segments

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### Competitive Positioning

Compares platforms based on order volume and average
order value, with revenue represented through bubble size.

### Revenue Market Share

Shows the relative revenue contribution of Blinkit,
Zepto and Swiggy Instamart.

### City Performance

The city matrix compares revenue contribution across
the top-performing cities and platforms.

### Discount Effectiveness

Compares average order value between discounted and
non-discounted orders across platforms.

### Delivery Performance

Analyzes the distribution of orders across different
delivery-time bands.

### Customer Experience

Examines the relationship between delivery speed
and customer satisfaction.

### Product Category Analysis

Uses a treemap to identify major product-category
revenue contributions.

### Geographic Analysis

Maps revenue and platform presence across cities.

### Customer Segmentation

Compares average order value across customer age groups.
